1.Preposition of time:
1) at : specific time, ex; at 10:00/ at noon/ at midnight
2) on: days, ex; on Monday, on christmas day, on my birthday
3) in: during time, ex; in the evening, in 1998, in winter, in christmas

2.Preposition of place
1) at: soecific location (at work, at school)
2) on: surface or media (on facebook, on internet)
3) in: inside (in the classroom, in the building)

a) unfinished action
	ex: Theo has been at school since september

	ex: I have never seen a gorilla
